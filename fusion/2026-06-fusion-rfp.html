Executive summary

Volume23 RFPs in June (20 real) — the strongest month of 2026, up ~4× from May (6). Unity is now the dominant source (23/24 Unity engine; source “Unity Asset Store”). The Unity collaboration is producing inbound.
Quality (the issue)Headline fields are filled (project overview, company, engine, a budget field — 96–100%), but the detail needed to quote is largely missing: needed resources 4%, asset quantity 4%, references 0–8%, and budgets are mostly placeholder. Result: 43% (10) went to NEED_ADDITIONAL_INFO. Most are “Short” RFPs (16/23) — the short intake produces thin briefs.
Outcome so far9 sent to client, 1 approved, 2 rejected, 1 still new (a duplicate). June deals are young — most are mid-funnel.
MixMostly RETAINER (18/24); disciplines skew 3D — characters, creatures, props, animation. Buyers are game studios.
TakeawayUnity drives volume — but the intake form lets thin RFPs through, costing a clarification round on 42%. This is the case for the AI-RFP / required-fields gate: capture resources, quantity, references and a real budget before submit.

Diagnosis
RFPs arrive with the “who/what” but not the “how much / to what standard.” The fields you need to actually quote and staff — resources, quantity, references, a real budget — are empty 90%+ of the time. That is exactly why 42% bounce to “need more info,” and it’s fixable at intake.

What users actually want
Mostly 3D characters / creatures, restyling & optimizing existing assets, plus some 2D key/cover art and shaders. Buyers are indie / solo devs on Unity, personal Gmail, budgets $50–1,500 (a few to $20k). Recurring motive: “so I don’t have to use AI” — they specifically want a human artist. That anti-AI demand is Fusion’s positioning.
6
Relevance & conclusions
are these the right requests?
Relevance is high
In the curated RFP data (2026), ~70% of scored RFPs are marked “relevant” (Yes 79 vs No 31), and June buyers are game studios on Unity — the right ICP. The inbound is good; the loss is in intake quality and follow-through, not in attracting wrong leads.
What to do
  • Fix intake (AI-RFP / required fields): make resources, asset quantity, references and a real budget required before submit — kills the 42% clarification round.
  • Lean into Unity: it’s now the #1 source — align supply (3D char/creature/prop) and the partnership reporting (v.10.12.0 UTM) to it.
  • Work the mid-funnel: 9 sent + 10 need-info — chase these to decision; don’t let June RFPs age.
